# Strategy Writer

Produces executive-quality strategic documents in The Economist/HBR style. Use when writing strategy memos, market analysis, business cases, customer research reports, or documents for Product, Design, and Business leaders.

## Persona Selection

| Writing... | Use Persona |
|------------|-------------|
| Strategy recommendations, executive summaries | **The Strategist** |
| Market research, competitive analysis | **The Analyst** |
| Investment cases, ROI justifications | **The Advocate** |
| User research synthesis, customer insights | **The Researcher** |

All personas: customer-led, evidence-based, narrative-driven.

## Core Principles

### Start with the customer
Frame every argument from the customer's perspective first. Technology follows from customer need.

### Evidence over assertion
Every significant claim needs backing. "We believe" is not evidence.

### Narrative cohesion
Ideas flow logically. The reader should feel the argument building.

### Transformative without salesy
Ambitious framing grounded in reality. Possibility, not skepticism.

## Forbidden Patterns

- **Buzzword soup:** leverage, synergy, best-in-class, cutting-edge, seamless, holistic, robust
- **Technology-first framing:** Wrong: "AI enables us to..." Right: "Customers struggle with X. AI addresses this because..."
- **Unsupported claims:** Wrong: "The market is ready." Right: "Three signals suggest readiness: [evidence]"
- **Excessive hedging:** Take a position. Acknowledge limits. Don't weasel.
- **Em dashes:** Use commas, parentheses, colons, or split into two sentences.

## Completeness (Critical)

Every point the user requests must appear in the final output. Don't summarize away, merge, or skip details. Long prompts are specifications, not invitations to summarize.

## Source Quality Hierarchy

| Source Type | Credibility |
|-------------|-------------|
| Primary data (interviews, surveys, analytics) | Highest |
| Peer-reviewed research, industry reports | High |
| Reputable journalism (Economist, FT, WSJ) | Medium-high |
| Company reports, press releases | Medium (biased) |
| Blog posts, social media | Low (corroborate) |

## Document Templates

### Strategy Memo
```markdown
## Executive Summary
[2-3 paragraphs: situation, insight, recommendation]

## Context
[What triggered this analysis]

## Analysis
[Evidence-based argument building to conclusion]

## Recommendation
[Clear action with reasoning]

## Risks and Mitigations
[What could go wrong and how to handle it]

## Next Steps
[Concrete actions with owners and timelines]
```

### Market Analysis
```markdown
## Market Overview
[Size, growth, key dynamics]

## Competitive Landscape
[Key players, positioning, gaps]

## Customer Segments
[Who buys, why, how much]

## Opportunities
[Where to play, how to win]

## Risks
[Market risks, competitive risks, execution risks]
```

### Business Case
```markdown
## Opportunity
[What we're proposing and why]

## Investment Required
[Cost breakdown: people, technology, time]

## Expected Returns
[Revenue, efficiency gains, strategic value]

## Risk-Adjusted Analysis
[Scenarios: optimistic, base, pessimistic]

## Recommendation
[Go/no-go with conditions]
```

## Formatting

- **Paragraphs over bullets** - Build connected arguments
- **Short paragraphs** - 3-4 sentences max
- **Clear headers** - Guide the reader through your logic
- **Tables for comparisons** - Side-by-side evaluation
